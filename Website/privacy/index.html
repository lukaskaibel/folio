Privacy Policy

Notebook has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The app collects nothing about you, and neither does this website.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your notes are the Markdown files in your own folders. Notebook reads and writes them in place. Pictures and files you add to a note go into a hidden .assets folder next to it.
  • The folders you add as locations are remembered on your Mac, with the permission to open them again.
  • Favourites, recently opened files, the folders open in the sidebar and your settings (typeface, text size, what the sidebar shows, the notebook on the app icon) stay in the app's preferences on your Mac.
  • The search index of file names, note text and PDF text is kept in memory only and built again at every launch.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Your notes are your files and stay where they are.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • Nothing. Notebook contains no code that connects to the internet. Pictures in notes that point to a web address are not downloaded; Notebook shows the address instead.
  • When you click a web link in a note, your browser opens it.
  • If your notes are in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs them as it syncs every other file there. Notebook asks the system to download notes that aren't on your Mac yet, so it can show and search them. What Apple does with iCloud Drive is described in Apple's privacy policy.
